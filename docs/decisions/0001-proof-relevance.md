# 0001: Proof-relevant identity

- **Status:** Accepted for Core v0.1
- **Date:** 2026-08-26

## Decision

Core identity types live in data-relevant universes. Core v0.1 has no special
proof-irrelevant <code>Prop</code> universe and no judgmental rule identifying
arbitrary inhabitants of the same type.

Identity is generated by reflexivity and eliminated by $J$.

## Reason

Homotopy type theory interprets identity proofs as paths. Distinct paths and
higher paths must not be collapsed by the kernel. Definitional proof
irrelevance would force path spaces to be propositions and defeat the intended
interpretation.

## Consequences

- Proof terms may affect types and computation.
- The runtime may not erase proof-like terms merely because they inhabit a
  syntactically distinguished universe.
- UIP and axiom K are not kernel principles.
- A later proof-irrelevant truncation must be an explicit construction or
  extension.
